## Core data model — extend canonical records, do not duplicate
1. **Company/client directory:** customer type, organization and multiple contacts/properties, landscape measurements/zones/assets, service preferences, communications/consent, lead source, retention milestones, seasonal service and signed contract history. Link existing CRM and Jobs records by IDs; show permission-safe cross-module timeline, before/after media, satisfaction/complaint outcomes, documents, warranty and service exceptions.
2. **Contracts:** one-time, weekly, biweekly, monthly, semiannual, annual, seasonal, per-storm, multi-year; effective dates, renewal options, escalation/cancellation/change control, pricing revisions, billing cadence, service visit cadence, weather hold and client approval; never assume a semiannual service visit from semiannual invoice cadence.
3. **Price and cost book:** units (labor-hour, square foot, cubic yard, linear foot, each/visit, per season), company-specific region/currency/tax, crew labor burden, materials, vendor rates, equipment operating cost, transport/disposal, overhead/risk/margin. No scraped competitor price is an approved customer quote. The example JSON catalog has unset prices on purpose.
4. **Job/project management:** estimate-to-job chain, Gantt timeline with baseline/current/actual milestones and durations, work breakdown and phases, site imagery, daily logs, time off, resource blockers, subcontractors, permit/utility locate compliance, change orders, QC, client signature, warranty/callbacks, earned margin and deposit/balance visibility.
5. **Dispatch calendar:** unified crew and equipment availability, holiday/vacation/sick/time-off blocks, job blocks and multi-day reservations, drag/drop in approved workflow, skills/certifications/shift compliance, weather/snow storm dispatch, conflict detection and before/after time estimates; all hard capacity/lockout boundaries from canonical authority.
6. **Employee HR:** scoped employee profile and emergency contact, onboarding/offboarding, employment status, job roles, credentials/certificates, wage and salary history with effective dates, overtime/payroll evidence, benefits, vacation balances, private incident and training records, performance feedback with human review and anti-bias protections. **Do not score workers from safety incidents or GPS traces.** Managers see only permission-appropriate information; wage history must not be in client payloads.
7. **Advanced Finance:** chart of accounts, accrual/cash configurable reporting, estimates, customer/project-specific deposits and retainer liability, progress/periodic invoices, recurring statements, credit notes, tax rates by province/state, A/R/A/P, GL posting review, bank reconciliation, cost codes, WIP/change-order forecast, subcontractor bills, inventory costing, payroll integration, cross-period close and immutable audit trails. Posting/payment execution remains independently authorized, double-entry validated, and idempotent.
8. **Client satisfaction:** opt-in private surveys, site reviews, complaint/callback action logs, service-quality checklists, response timeliness and retention. No automatic public review publication or inferred sentiments.
9. **OSHA / Ontario OHSA:** jurisdiction- and activity-aware; US landscaping maintenance vs construction OSHA standard families differ. Ontario employer training, PPE fit, policies and worksite duties derive from OHSA; future modules need hazard controls for heat/cold, trenching/utility locates, electrical, pesticides, chainsaws, chippers, tree work, vehicle backup and fatigue, seasonal snow/ice work, high-risk construction. Sources: https://www.osha.gov/landscaping/hazards/ ; https://www.osha.gov/landscaping/standards ; https://www.ontario.ca/document/guide-occupational-health-and-safety-act/part-iii-duties-employers-and-other-persons
10. **Help/learning system:** a circled information icon adjacent to every section/complex field, searchable Help articles with screenshots, workflows, glossary, empty-state hints, keyboard accessibility and mobile support, and role-aware guidance that never discloses hidden company data.
11. **Company billing SaaS (future):** platform organization != landscaping customer's CRM record. Onboard tenant with separate logo/domain/tax/timezone/currency/company contacts. Every company-owned row, join, RPC, storage object, search result, PDF export, realtime stream, background job and cache requires tenant isolation. Use strict authenticated membership plus audited RLS; never trust caller-provided tenant IDs or user-editable claims. No cross-tenant aggregate or shared service keys in browser.
12. **Subscription entitlements (future):** platform-admin-managed module availability per company from signed active subscription and recorded plan/version; plan upgrade/downgrade with audit, add-on flags, seat limits, storage budgets, throttling/quota warning, soft suspension and read-only export/grace. **UI flags alone are not authorization**: gate RPC/storage, role permission, subscription entitlement and tenant for every read/write. Actual subscription checkout and webhook billing need separate security/finance release. Price tiers and limits are not yet live commitments.
13. **Template and demo:** Yard Workers as an owner-controlled demo with deterministic synthetic clients/jobs/contracts/assets/employee roles, not actual customer identities or invented amounts. Resettable fixture data in a separate isolated company only after security tests. Distinguish owner platform admin from tenant company admin, tenant employee, customer, accountant and auditor. Company trial must not use Production customer records.

## SaaS security release prerequisites
- Review all existing tables/views/RPCs/Edge Functions for company ownership and bypass-RLS behavior; no bulk company_id injection without complete joins and policies.
- Tenant A / Tenant B / suspended tenant / non-member / guest allow-deny matrices for SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE, files, search, events, jobs, deposits and exports.
- Use source-effective roles, server-validated plan state, secure webhooks, explicit storage bucket policies, encryption and PII audit; do not copy existing production records as a demo.
- Controlled staged migration and reversible backfill with zero lost ownership links; existing Yard Workers single-company access remains intact until migration is proven.
- Starter/Professional/Enterprise are product concepts, **not** approved subscription prices, processing fees, tax treatment or operational entitlements.
