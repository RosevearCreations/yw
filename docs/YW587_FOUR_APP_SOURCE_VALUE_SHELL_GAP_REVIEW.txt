## Product separation: four independent financial systems
- **Yard Workers:** each subscribing landscaping company's ledger, AR/AP, payroll, jobs, invoices, advance/holdback, HST and Canadian tax/GIFI workpapers remain solely within its tenant accounting boundaries. Rosevear Shell gets anonymized technical health, NOT per-tenant financial rights.
- **iCamp:** each campground owns stays, deposits, revenue earned by site-night, guest folio, campsite/rental inventory, seasonal agreements, tax and close.
- **Devil n Dove:** maker owns raw-material lots, work in progress, scrap/yields, finished pieces, consignment/market/offline stock, commissions, production costing and sales accounting.
- **Rosie Dazzlers:** detailer owns appointment and retail revenue, mileage/van/consumables, deposits/tips, pickup/stand, refunds, settlement and route contribution.
- **FC01–10 / CA01–10:** comparable *outcomes* (balanced postings, individual chart, independent merchant credentials, reconciled bank, P&L/balance sheet/cash flow, audit/close, Canada GIFI/HST/workpapers and proper LLC/jurisdiction profile if applicable), **not shared ledger tables or shared payment keys**. No Master can book a sale or refund.

- **P2 accessibility:** standard Yard Workers blue theme retained; operator ownership/next action readable on mobile, keyboard navigation, time zone and long tenant name, no status-only colour, accessible redacted case references.
- **Acceptance gate:** Shell Review pp. 6–7 five stages: baseline, one observable tenant, complete missing-photo loop, restore/offline-Master continuity, witnessed production and measured SLA. A rehearsal closes only its own rehearsal milestone.
