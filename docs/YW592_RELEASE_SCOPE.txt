LIVE FUNCTIONAL CONTRACT (only after migration, Edge and secrets are deployed):
1. Original authenticated company owner/reviewer may triage private YW591
   photo attempts, expired leases, verified receipts and quarantined cases.
   No public image, customer identifier or device-local content in results.
2. Only owner proposes a bounded 30/90-day retention, expiry review or
   legal-hold review against an existing original attempt. Only the already
   approved different reviewer can sign within 24 hours. An HMAC-SHA256
   server signature covers exact proposal, original author, reviewer,
   original byte digest, action, reason, bounded date and timestamp.
   Readback verifies the stored immutable signature. It is a server
   integrity signature PLUS independently authenticated human approval;
   not a qualified legal electronic signature.
3. Expiry means ELIGIBLE FOR REVIEW, NOT DELETE. No SQL or Edge function
   deletes evidence, uploads photos publicly or changes finance records.
   Original YW591 attempt and approved tenant scope cannot be bypassed.
4. Original enrolled crew, SAME original uploader, verified source ledger
   and independent private Storage SHA-256 byte re-download are mandatory
   before source metadata may be sent to a configured Master receiver.
   Outbound payload is HMAC signed; no raw bytes/customer PII. Receiver
   must return matching nonce, source payload digest, unique receipt ID
   and HMAC. ACK is verified independently BEFORE immutable receipt insert.
   Transport success/HTTP 200 alone is NEVER a durable ACK.
5. Field readiness returns only the number of independently consented
   real original companies with separate owner/reviewer and valid source
   Master receipt. At least TWO different real companies are needed.
   Even two source receipts do NOT constitute observed physical device
   field certification. Fixtures are not real pilot witnesses.
